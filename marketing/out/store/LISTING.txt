DESCRIPTION
30% Off EVERYTHING takes 30% off the entire internet. Literally: on Black Friday it covers the right-hand 30% of every web page with a giant sale sign.

• Automatic on Black Friday, midnight to midnight, your time.
• Click the toolbar button any other day to preview it on the current page. Click again to hide it.
• It is a joke. It does not find coupons or change prices.
• Collects nothing, sends nothing, no account.

PRIVACY PRACTICES TAB

Single purpose
Shows a novelty sale banner over the right-hand 30% of web pages on Black Friday, or when the toolbar button is clicked.

activeTab justification
Lets the toolbar button add the banner to the current tab when that tab was open before the extension was installed or updated.

scripting justification
Used only by that toolbar click, to inject the extension's own banner script into the current tab. No remote code.

Host permission justification (content script on all URLs)
The banner is the whole product and must appear on every page on Black Friday. It reads nothing from the page and makes no network requests.
