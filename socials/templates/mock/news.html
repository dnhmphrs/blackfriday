Shoppers brace for the busiest day of the year as retailers promise their deepest cuts yet

Analysts expect record traffic online, with almost every large site running a sitewide promotion.

By our retail correspondent · 6 min read

High streets opened early this morning, though most of the day's spending is expected to happen on screens. Retailers have spent weeks trailing their offers, and several said they had doubled server capacity in anticipation.

Consumer groups repeated their annual advice to compare prices before buying, noting that a discount is only a discount if the starting figure was real. "Look at what you actually get," one adviser said.

Delivery firms have taken on thousands of seasonal staff. Parcel volumes are forecast to peak early next week, when the weekend's orders work their way through sorting centres across the country.

Economists are watching the figures closely for signs of how households are faring. Spending on small luxuries has held up better than expected this year, while larger purchases have been postponed.

For many shoppers the appeal is simpler. "I wait all year for this," said one, refreshing a basket she had filled three days earlier. "Everything is cheaper. That is the whole point of today."
