DESCRIPTION  (public)
---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
30% off the entire internet.
Every website. Black Friday only.

A Black Friday novelty for your browser. On Black Friday it brings the 30% OFF sale to every website you visit, from midnight to midnight in your time zone. The rest of the year it does nothing.

A Gary Mustard Enterprises production.

---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
PRIVACY PRACTICES TAB  (seen by the reviewer only)
---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Single purpose
Displays a decorative sale banner on web pages on Black Friday only.

Host permission justification
The content script runs on all http and https pages because the banner appears on every page on Black Friday. On any other day it checks the date and stops. It does not read page content, store data or make network requests.

Remote code
No, I am not using remote code.

Data usage
Tick nothing. Tick the three certifications.

Privacy policy URL
Leave blank.

---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
TEST INSTRUCTIONS TAB  (seen by the reviewer only)
---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The extension is active on Black Friday only. To test: set the computer's date to Friday 27 November 2026, then open any website. A banner covers the right-hand 30% of the page (the top third on windows narrower than 769px). On any other date the extension does nothing.
